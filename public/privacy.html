Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2025  ·  Last updated: 1 June 2025

We keep this policy short and plain-language. We collect only what we need to run the Service and never sell your data.

1. Who We Are

EaglercraftXLauncher is operated by CK Gaming Studios. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you use our platform at eagxlauncher.pages.dev.

2. Data We Collect

Data Source Why we collect it
Email address Google / GitHub OAuth Account identity, deduplication across providers
Display name Google / GitHub OAuth Shown on your public profile and uploaded content
OAuth provider ID Google / GitHub OAuth Linking your account to the OAuth provider securely
Profile bio & Gravatar email Provided voluntarily by you Personalise your public profile
Uploaded content Provided by you Hosting and displaying clients, mods, and skins
Session token Generated on login Keeping you logged in (stored in your browser's sessionStorage)
Basic request logs Cloudflare infrastructure Security, abuse prevention, debugging

We do not collect passwords — authentication is handled entirely by Google/GitHub.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate and maintain your account and profile.
  • To display your uploaded content and attribute it to your username.
  • To send session tokens so you remain authenticated.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, spam, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To respond to support requests you initiate.

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Data Storage

Your account data and content metadata are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV, distributed globally across Cloudflare's edge network. Uploaded client HTML files are stored as assets on GitHub Releases in the EaglercraftXLauncher repository.

Session tokens are stored only in your browser's sessionStorage and are never written to a persistent cookie.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare — cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • GitHub — GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google OAuth — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Gravatar (Automattic) — automattic.com/privacy

6. Cookies & Local Storage

EaglercraftXLauncher does not use tracking cookies. We use browser sessionStorage to hold your authentication token for the duration of your browser session. This data is cleared automatically when you close your browser tab.

Eaglercraft clients running inside the launcher may use IndexedDB and localStorage to store game save data. This data is namespaced per client and stored entirely in your own browser — we have no access to it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update your name, bio, and Gravatar email from your profile page.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact us through the platform.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Session tokens expire automatically after 30 days. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain it.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy should be directed to us via our GitHub repository.
